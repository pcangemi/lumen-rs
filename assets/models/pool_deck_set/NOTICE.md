The original full mesh is converted from GLB to OBJ/MTL, with its source texture
pixels retained, grounded and centered in meters. Roughness and metallic factors
are mapped to Lumen's materials. Degenerate source faces are ignored by the importer.
No decimation is applied. Source checksums: ../coastal-sources.json.
