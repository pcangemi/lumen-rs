# Arc Floor Lamp

Floor Lamp by tevy.dev, released under CC0 1.0.

Source: https://tevy.dev/models/floor-lamp
Pinned GLB: https://tevy.dev/models/floor-lamp/v1/lod0.glb
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Converted to Y-up OBJ/MTL; compressed KTX2 maps decoded using Khronos KTX-Software.
Packed roughness and metallic channels are stored separately. Original geometry,
UVs and normals are retained. The scene scales the model and assigns the bulb
warm emission for the night preset. This is a generic source model, not a
manufacturer-certified replica.

The apartment extends the source cord from its measured endpoint to a modeled
plug and wall outlet; the original source mesh remains intact.
Only the cylindrical cloth shade faces receive diffuse transmission; the marble
base, metal support and original texture maps retain their other material settings.
