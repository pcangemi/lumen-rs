The rug uses the 1K diffuse, OpenGL normal, roughness and 16-bit displacement maps from [Curly Teddy Natural](https://polyhaven.com/a/curly_teddy_natural), by Rico Cilliers and colormass, under CC0. The displacement map produces actual centimeter-spaced backing relief. Original short boucle cords add rounded geometry and self-shadowing, with the source color, roughness and a softened normal map on their surfaces. This remains a surface model rather than a fiber scattering simulation. Local files are `textures/rug-color.jpg`, `textures/rug-normal.jpg`, `textures/rug-roughness.jpg` and `textures/rug-height.png`.

`models/sources.json` pins the original model download URLs and MD5 checksums (published by Poly Haven; locally recorded for the pinned Khronos revision). Models are converted to meters and Y-up coordinates, centered horizontally and grounded at their lowest point. Packed roughness/metallic channels are split into linear scalar PNGs; the original UVs and split normals are retained. No source geometry is subdivided merely to inflate polygon counts.

To reproduce the conversions with Blender installed:

```bash
python3 scripts/fetch-interior-assets.py
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --python scripts/prepare-interior-assets.py
```

The cabinet's table lamp, curtain rods, window joinery, radiator sections and rug edge are original procedural project geometry. The pendant and arc lamp sources are credited below. The Poly Haven furniture remains CC0; the project license covers only its original contributions.

The plant is [Potted Plant 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_02) by Rico Cilliers (CC0), including its leaf color, normal, roughness and opacity maps. Opacity cutouts affect camera, indirect and shadow rays.

The modern sofa is [GlamVelvetSofa](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/GlamVelvetSofa), by Eric Chadwick, copyright 2021 Wayfair, LLC, under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See [its attribution and modification notice](models/glam_velvet_sofa/NOTICE.md). It is an existing product model with adapted cream upholstery; the renderer does not yet reproduce the original velvet sheen extension.

## Day and night apartment

- [Rattan Pendant Light](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/ceiling-lights-and-chandeliers) by vinish.dev, **CC BY 4.0**; [conversion notice](models/rattan_pendant/NOTICE.md). The source uses an opacity-masked weave. The apartment replaces that shell with physical interlaced strands following its radial profile; its original fittings and bulb are retained.
- [Floor Lamp](https://tevy.dev/models/floor-lamp) by tevy.dev, **CC0 1.0**; [conversion notice](models/arc_floor_lamp/NOTICE.md). Both lamps retain their existing bulb meshes. SHA-256 hashes and download URLs are in `models/lighting-sources.json`.
- Claude Monet, *Water Lilies* (1906), Art Institute of Chicago, accession 1933.1157. [Museum record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/16568/water-lilies); [public-domain/CC0 reproduction on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Claude_Monet_-_Water_Lilies_-_1933.1157_-_Art_Institute_of_Chicago.jpg). The image is mapped at its original aspect ratio onto a shallow canvas mesh, without repainting or generative changes.

The irregular curtains, frame and individual rug yarn loops
are original project geometry. The loops are a surface approximation; they do
not use a dedicated wool-fiber scattering model. Source material radiance is
chosen for this scene and is not a certified photometric measurement.

To restore the two lamp conversions, run Blender with
`--background --python scripts/prepare-lighting-assets.py`. Set `KTX_TOOL` to the
Khronos KTX-Software `ktx` executable to decode the arc lamp's source textures.
The renderer uses the bundled conversions and needs neither Blender nor KTX at runtime.

The white painted timber windows are original procedural geometry, using the
traditional rounded meeting stiles, crémone hardware and glazing-bead construction
illustrated by [Atulam's heritage windows](https://www.atulam.fr/fenetres/tradilou-haussmannienne/)
as a construction reference. No manufacturer mesh or image is redistributed.
The cabinet lamp is original geometry with a separate bulb and a diffuse-transmitting
shade. Both floor-level lamp leads connect to modeled wall outlets.

The opposite masonry facade, recessed rooms, window glazing, shutters, mansard
roof, dormers, chimney stacks, downpipes and public street luminaires are original
procedural project geometry, informed by the [UNESCO Paris heritage atlas](https://whc.unesco.org/en/urban-heritage-atlas/Paris).
They reuse the credited plaster and linen maps. The imported ironwork is credited below.

- **Juliet Balcony** and **Wrought Iron Balcony**, by vinish.dev, [source models](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/balconies-and-railings), **CC BY 4.0**. ## Coastal villa

- **Modern four-bedroom villa with pool**, [Innerscene](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/modern-four-bedroom-villa-with-pool-3d-model-ec51544e), **CC0**. The source building, interior rooms, doors, glazing and fittings are retained. Its original pool and timber deck are replaced by this project's tiled basin and terrace. Materials are adapted to pale mineral plaster, stone and timber; small construction bevels and a timber-lined canopy are added. This is an adapted architectural model, not a scan of a Californian property.
- **Pool Deck Set** and **Canopy Daybed**, [vinish.dev](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/outdoor-furniture), **CC BY 4.0**. Original full source meshes and texture pixels; see their bundled modification notices.
- **Outdoor Table Chair Set 01**, **Coast Line 02**, **Grass Bermuda 01**, **Potted Plant 04**, **Quiver Tree 01** and **Shrub 04**, [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models), **CC0**. Full source geometry, 2K source maps and original UVs/normals. Grass variants are exported individually and scattered. Separate source opacity maps restore the grass and shrub cutouts omitted by their glTF exports. The coastline preserves all 756,940 source triangles. Source plant scales and placement are adapted for the landscape.
- **White Sandstone Blocks 02**, **White Plaster 02**, **Grass Ground**, **Oak Wood Planks** and **Concrete Floor Worn 001**, [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/textures), **CC0**. Original 2K color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. The scene adjusts material tint and color-map blend strength; source pixels are not repainted.
- The photographed sky and tea set reuse the sources credited above.

Individual source links, pinned file URLs and SHA-256 checksums are in
[`models/coastal-sources.json`](models/coastal-sources.json). Reproduction:
`scripts/prepare-coastal-assets.py`, executed with Blender. It preserves source
mesh detail, extracts packed linear data channels and folds exactly constant
channels into scalar material factors. It does not decimate or resize the assets.

The blue and gold mosaic, dolphin silhouette, tiled steps, coping, paving,
landscape layout and wave geometry are original project work. The architectural
composition is informed by the [Carmel Beach House by MIST/EYRC](https://www.mistarchitects.com/carmelbeachhouse),
particularly its deep overhangs, mineral palette and connection between glazing,
terrace and water. No reference photographs or architectural drawings are redistributed.
